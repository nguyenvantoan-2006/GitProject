THE QUIET ATLAS
A field guide to finding your way
Linh Nguyễn

CHAPTER ONE — THE SPACE BETWEEN

Before you decide where to go, let yourself arrive where you are.

Put both feet on the floor. Notice the weight of your hands. There is no need to turn this moment into a lesson or a plan. For now, it is enough to be here, in the small and ordinary space between one thing and the next.

Try this: take one unhurried breath. Name one thing you can see, one thing you can hear, and one thing you are ready to set down for today.

The map begins with noticing.
